# Participation

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the work and its evidence;
avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language and unwanted contact.
Respect people's privacy and do not publish their personal information or
browsing activity.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict
participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to
security@marcode.ai. Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and consider
the context and impact when responding.
